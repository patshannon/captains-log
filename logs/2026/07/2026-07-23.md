# Captain's Log — 2026-07-23
Heavy day on private-project-1 — the feed is starting to feel like an actual product.

The two big PRs tell the story. First, shipping a public `/watch/[id]` route with a Share-to sheet on the feed rail. The key design decision there was moving away from slug-based URLs to bare entity IDs — slugs are still accepted for backwards compatibility, but nothing new emits them. Cleaner links, easier to generate, no fragility around slug collisions or renames. The share sheet itself lives on the feed rail, so it's a one-tap surface without pulling users out of context.

The second PR was tap-to-pause with a "Step In" overlay on every feed card. This is actually a meaningful UX pivot — renaming the Play pill to "Step In" is a signal about what the product *is*. It's not passive playback, it's participation. Dropped the like/heart stub from the action rail at the same time, which I'd been wanting to do; that thing was placeholder code that had overstayed its welcome and made the rail feel unfinished every time I looked at it.

On top of the PRs, a handful of cleanup commits that mattered more than their size suggests. The onboarding interests step was silently broken — it had been rendering stale data since the replay-feed refactor changed the defaults, and nobody caught it because it's early in the funnel and not exercised by most test paths. Fixed it by binding to the fixed `STORY_CATEGORIES` catalog directly. The "Soon" stubs for Explore nav and Follow pill are also gone now; stub UI that ships but does nothing erodes trust faster than a missing feature does.

A few polish passes too — full-bleeding the Share-to channel row to fix scroll-end clipping, standardizing nav icon slot sizes so Create and avatar chips actually align with the stroke icons on both breakpoints, and pulling the dead `/play/cms` hrefs that had been silently going nowhere.

The feed is getting coherent. A lot of what shipped today was about removing things that didn't belong rather than adding things that did.
