# Captain's Log — 2026-07-03
Two distinct workstreams today, both substantial in different ways.

The bigger lift on the private-project-2 side was getting two feature PRs out the door simultaneously — a lawn timeline with score trends and treatment logging, and a shareable score card. That's a lot of surface area to be cutting at once, and the bug trail on the share feature shows it. The OG image route needed to 404 properly and be opted out of the framework's default route caching — the kind of thing that only bites you when you realize a stale cached response is serving as the social preview for a card that no longer exists. The photo opt-in reset on revoke was a straightforward correctness fix, but the concurrent revoke race condition was trickier: if a token gets deleted mid-request by a concurrent revoke, you don't want to report back that a live token exists. Small window, real consequence, and the kind of edge case that's easy to miss until you reason carefully about the lifecycle of a share link.

The timeline fix was simpler but important — anchoring a treatment date to the user's local calendar day rather than UTC. This class of bug is so common and so easy to introduce. Server timestamps in UTC, user sees an event shifted by a day depending on their timezone. Two files changed, but it's the kind of fix that meaningfully improves trust in the data.

Over on private-project-1, the voiced playthrough replay feature landed — 41 files changed, which signals this was a genuine architectural addition rather than a patch. Pre-rendering TTS audio for replays is an interesting tradeoff: you give up freshness and pay a storage/generation cost upfront, but you eliminate latency at playback time and get deterministic output. For a replay experience that's probably being consumed in a low-latency-tolerance context, that's the right call. Integrating pre-rendered audio into a replay pipeline means threading it through whatever timing and sequencing system drives the playthrough, which is nontrivial. Glad that one shipped.

Busy day. Both PRs are open and the fixes are merged — tomorrow is probably review and iteration territory.
