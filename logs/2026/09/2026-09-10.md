# Captain's Log — 2026-09-10
Today was a big push on the writer’s loop across `private-project-1` — twenty-five commits that landed the foundation for segment-based script editing in the Forge. The short version: we’re moving from a monolithic story-editing surface into a structured, beat-by-beat composition flow, and most of the core machinery is now in place.

The day’s work breaks into a few clear arcs. First, the cold open got its Forge treatment — that’s the introductory segment where the player first encounters the story. I wired up the “Intro” stage as a rail component (the horizontal progression widget) and removed the in-run gear icon from individual shots, since editing now happens in a dedicated context. Then I extended the same pattern to Segment 1, bringing its scripted beats into the Forge and nesting them under the Intro’s shots. The strip metaphors started clicking here: each stage’s strips are the “one home” for those scripts, handling first shot creation, beat fixes, and deletions. The Edit story form lost its two script fields in the process, which felt right — the form is for metadata now, and the scripts live where they belong.

There’s a solid chunk of player-side work too. The choice surface got reordered to match the server’s sequence, and the endorse instrument was pushed off the player’s paths entirely — the choice rendering now comes from the API directly, which means the player no longer needs to maintain its own ordering logic.

The gear icon editing flow got a proper implementation: clicking it opens the beat as fields — scene description, lines, choices, notices — with a “Save & Regenerate” primary verb that kicks off an AI take under “More options.” This is the L1 slice of the writer’s loop, and it’s paired with a Forge review route (L4) that runs at 372’s speed (whatever that winds up meaning in practice — the docs reference a specific API pin).

On the infrastructure side, I added a `RequireWriter` auth gate to the `/forge` layout with a test that pins the player’s app, and tweaked the shell so the writer’s desktop columns completely leave the play layout (D31). There’s also a small fix in the Forge’s doc PUT handler — when a `change` with `kind: "generation"` arrives without a `fields` property, we now accept it gracefully instead of choking. Two tickets (A47, A48) came out of that.
