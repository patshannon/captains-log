# Captain's Log — 2026-09-11
Heavy day in the Forge project — 33 commits across the writer, player, audio, and shelf modules, plus a handful of merges to keep feature branches in sync. The work was largely about converging the UX: pulling editing into the Forge workspace, streamlining the intake and the shelf, and fixing a batch of usability issues that surfaced during live runs.

The biggest arc was moving cast and place editing into the Forge story stage, out of the standalone story editor dialog. This simplified the data flow: the writer edits characters and locations inside the same document view where they'll later prepare the show. The story editor still exists but now only holds narrative text; the cast and places strips sit beneath the stage strip in the Forge workspace. I also opened that story editor as a centered dialog within the bounded viewport, fixed a focus-outline clipping issue, and added republish reminders — small but necessary papercuts.

On the preparation side, I wired the intro segment into the one-shot "Prepare" flow that fires automatically on arrival from Apply. Every stage now shows a status (done / generating / incomplete), and the bundle tap exits the rail cleanly. The preflight intake was reworked to show a single card without the feedback chain or onboarding clutter — writers land directly on the shelf now, no interstitial splash. That felt good to delete.
