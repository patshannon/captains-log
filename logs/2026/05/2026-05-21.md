# Captain's Log — 2026-05-21
Today was essentially a parity audit day — methodical, unglamorous, and exactly the kind of work that prevents a slow drift into incompatibility.

The core thread running through almost everything was a systematic comparison against a reference CMS implementation, using it as the source of truth to surface gaps in the current codebase. The first order of business was formalizing that comparison: writing reference docs and a proper gap analysis across 15 files. Having that written down transformed what could have been ad hoc fixes into a structured checklist. Everything that followed was working through that checklist.

The fixes themselves spanned several distinct subsystems. The profile section ("Mon profil") needed a handful of corrections to bring its behavior into alignment — likely field handling or form logic that had diverged. The personal space view ("Mon Espace") had pagination and category ID mismatches, the kind of subtle inconsistency that's invisible until you're doing a side-by-side audit and realize two things that should be identical aren't. The email change flow required more substantial work — 10 files touched — suggesting the gap there was architectural rather than cosmetic. And the under-14 to over-14 account upgrade path needed its own alignment pass.

One small but satisfying fix: correcting a `PageSize` type mismatch on the `UserLists` component. Type drift between a component's local definition and the canonical shared type is exactly the category of bug that's silent until it isn't, and catching it during an audit rather than in production is the whole point of doing audits.

On the documentation side, I trimmed `AGENTS.md` down to prescriptive guidance only and cleaned out a batch of migration-era docs that had been accumulating. Thirty-eight files changed in that cleanup commit — a reminder of how much dead weight accretes over time. The goal was to leave only instructions that tell an agent (or a human) exactly what to do, with nothing ambiguous or historical cluttering the signal.

Clean foundation. Tomorrow can actually build on it.
