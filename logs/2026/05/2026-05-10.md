# Captain's Log — 2026-05-10
Spent most of today in pixel-pushing territory — the kind of UI alignment work that sounds trivial until you're staring at a two-pixel spacing discrepancy between a redesigned component and its legacy counterpart, wondering why it bothers you so much.

The work centered on reading list cards in a private project: getting title spacing and overall card layout to match what the legacy system produces. This is the unglamorous side of incremental UI migration. You're not building something new, you're threading a needle between "good enough for now" and "consistent enough to ship" — and the legacy baseline is the spec, whether you like it or not. Three separate commits to sort out spacing suggests I went back and forth a bit, which tracks. These things rarely land clean on the first pass.

The other change was more interesting in a different way: hardcoding a subjects endpoint on the homepage. That kind of decision is usually a deliberate short-circuit — skip the dynamic config, the feature flag, or the service discovery layer and just wire it directly. It's technical debt by design, the kind you take on consciously to unblock something else. Worth noting so future-me remembers this was intentional and not an oversight.

Light day overall, but sometimes that's what the work demands — steady, careful, unexciting progress.
