# Captain's Log — 2026-05-08
Light day. One commit across one private project — a small fix touching French localization text and some icon handling logic in a `ShowcaseCard` component.

Two characters of changed text and a couple of guard clauses can take longer to get right than a hundred lines of new feature code. Today was probably one of those days.
