# Captain's Log — 2026-05-07
Signup flow day. All five commits landed in the same private project, and the theme was consistent: getting a rebuilt registration experience to behave exactly like the legacy system it's replacing.

The bulk of the work was parity work — the unglamorous but necessary kind. Two commits tackled the over-14 signup path and GTM tracking/consent fields specifically, making sure the new implementation matches the legacy behavior closely enough that nothing regresses from an analytics or compliance standpoint. Consent field parity in particular is the sort of thing that's easy to get subtly wrong: field names, event shapes, and the order of operations around when tracking fires all have to line up, otherwise you're looking at broken funnels or, worse, miscaptured consent state.

The nullable teacher agreements change was a small but meaningful schema decision. Making those fields optional rather than required reflects a real product reality — not every signup flow goes through a teacher-specific path — and getting that wrong at the model layer tends to cause validation errors that are annoying to debug later. Better to be explicit about optionality upfront.

Rounding it out were a couple of smaller fixes: updating last login on auth sign-in (one of those things that seems obvious but is easy to miss in a rewrite) and a layout fix for the signup confirmation screen. Nothing dramatic, but confirmation views are often the most neglected part of a flow and they're what users actually see last.

Overall it was a focused day with a clear throughline. Parity work like this isn't the most exciting engineering, but it's important — cutting over to a new signup implementation without it is how you introduce quiet regressions that take weeks to surface. Solid progress.
