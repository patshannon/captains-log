# Captain's Log — 2026-08-24
Sundays are for decompression. Zero commits, zero PRs — just a push to keep the log current. I've been mulling over the GraphQL vs. REST decision for the API layer we're starting on Monday. There's something tempting about the flexibility of GraphQL, especially since the client team keeps asking for nested data that REST would force us to fragment across endpoints. But I keep coming back to the operational simplicity of REST — caching is a solved problem, observability is mature, and I've seen too many GraphQL resolvers turn into N+1 query mines the moment you add a handful of fields.

I also spent some time this morning reviewing my notes on the Postgres partitioning approach for the new analytics tables. The range partitioning on the `created_at` column looks solid, but I'm still not sold on the default partition strategy for out-of-range data. Might just skip the default and let an audit job catch stragglers instead — cleaner than silently swallowing bad timestamps into a catch-all bucket.

The break has been good. I'm re-reading a bit on event-driven architecture patterns, mostly the outbox pattern, and I think there's a real opportunity to apply it to the order processing pipeline rather than relying on dual-writes or distributed transactions. Feels like the right time to make that call before we scope out the service boundaries.

Monday's going to be a long one. At least the coffee's good.
