# Captain's Log — 2026-08-03
Spent the day doing something I should have done a few weeks ago: a proper pass through my resume to align it with where I actually want to position myself right now.

The core of the work was stripping out AI tooling references that no longer reflect how I'm spending my time or what I want to be talking about in interviews. Résumés drift — you add things when they're exciting or relevant, and then the landscape shifts and suddenly you've got a skills section that reads like a different chapter of your career. Better to be intentional about the story than to leave noise in there for a recruiter to anchor on.

The more substantive change was reorienting the overall narrative around platform modernization. That's genuinely the through-line of my recent work — the hard, unsexy, high-leverage effort of moving systems forward: modernizing infrastructure, reducing tech debt, making things maintainable at scale. The header and summary sections got updated to reflect that framing, and the experience bullets got tightened to reinforce rather than dilute it.

The pull request workflow here is a little ceremonial for a solo project, but I find it useful to have a clean diff to review before merging changes to something as high-stakes as a resume. Easy to miss a subtle word choice that changes the tone of an entire section when you're editing inline.

Small day overall, but this is the kind of maintenance work that compounds. A resume that tells a coherent story is worth the hour it takes to prune it.
