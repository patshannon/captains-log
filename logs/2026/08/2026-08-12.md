# Captain's Log — 2026-08-12
A day of plumbing and patching — the kind where you fix one bug and uncover two more lurking in the same pipe. On private-project-1, the main event was a double-fire in the publish pipeline: every `_PUBLISH_` document was triggering its own `onWrite` listener, which in turn wrote back to the document and re-triggered the listener. That’s a ~2x cost per publish, not to mention the race conditions in status updates. The fix was a guard — check if the update came from our own system before acting. The same branch also disables the patch endpoints entirely (they were a footgun) and corrects error serialization in the marc worker so a malformed record doesn’t swallow the whole batch. The sweep script is a one‑time reset to unstick documents that had `patching:true` left behind from the broken listener — a fine example of “write the migration, then fix the bug that made it necessary.”

I also added a scheduled Airtable sync: runs every two hours between 7am and 7pm Montreal time, production only. Just a single Cloud Scheduler + Cloud Function, but it saves me a manual “download CSV, import, pray” ritual that was eating my afternoon every other day. Feels good to automate that.

On private-project-2, the Bachelorette TV research‑alignment pass went in alongside ceremony flow fixes. The prototype branch `prototype/replayable-cinematic-challenges` suggests we’re thinking about how to make challenge scenes replayable without breaking narrative continuity — a tricky design space. The ceremony flow fixes address edge cases around elimination timing and camera triggers. I spent more time reading than writing on that one, but the mental model is solidifying.

All told, a satisfying mix of defensive fixes, operational automation, and narrative-system design. Not every day needs a new feature.
