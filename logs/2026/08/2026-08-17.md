Light day today — mostly a single meaningful commit on the private project, and a couple of pushes that were really just syncing branches rather than real work.

The commit worth noting is an update to the admin UI’s pipeline reliability handoff. I tweaked the implementation details and adjusted the staging gate status semantics. This was driven by a subtle mismatch I noticed last week: the handoff between the staging gate and the next pipeline stage was treating a passing gate as a greenlight to proceed immediately, but in practice we need a brief validation window before we consider the handoff complete. Without that window, we risk letting flaky metrics slip through the cracks — the classic "gate passed but we're not actually ready" problem.

The change is small — one file — but it clarifies the contract. The staging gate now reports its status more precisely, distinguishing between "gate has completed evaluating" and "gate has verified the downstream is ready to accept." It's one of those distinctions that feels pedantic until you see it cause a production scare.
